A voice can sound real without being real.

Voice cloning rebuilds a person’s timbre from seconds of speech. Text-to-speech systems generate fluent audio from nothing. Voice conversion turns one speaker into another. Replay attacks play a genuine recording back through a speaker. Each leaves different traces, and none of them announce themselves to a listener.

Voice cloning
A model learns a target speaker’s voice and speaks new sentences in it.
Synthetic speech
Text-to-speech output with no human speaker behind it.
Voice conversion
A real performance is re-voiced so it sounds like someone else.
Replay
A genuine recording is played into a microphone, adding room and speaker traces.
Manipulated audio
Real speech edited, spliced or processed to change its meaning.

See the signal the way the model does.

Waveform, spectrogram, pitch and energy describe the same moment of speech from different angles.

The model reads frequency structure over time. Smoothness in the spectrogram, unnatural pitch micro-variation and missing high-band detail are cues, never verdicts on their own.

Data structures that do the work.

Each one has a job in the pipeline.

  • Sliding window

    Cuts audio into overlapping segments for scoring.

  • Queue / deque

    Buffers incoming live chunks in order.

  • Priority queue

    Surfaces the highest-priority alerts first.

  • Trie

    Finds keywords in the transcript as it grows.

  • String matching

    Detects multi-word patterns in context.

  • Hash map

    Looks up sessions, models and metadata in constant time.

  • Sorting

    Orders history and timeline events.

  • Binary search

    Maps a timestamp to its window instantly.

What AV-ASOC cannot tell you.

  • Model uncertainty

    Scores are probabilities, not proof.

  • Unknown generators

    New synthesis methods may not resemble training data.

  • Noise and compression

    Phone codecs and background noise can mask or mimic artifacts.

  • Short samples

    A few seconds carry little evidence.

  • False positives

    Genuine speech can be flagged.

  • False negatives

    Convincing fakes can pass.
